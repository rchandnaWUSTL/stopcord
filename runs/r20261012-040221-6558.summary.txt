All tests pass now. The issue was in `src/shop/pricing.py` - the `apply_discount` function was incorrectly dividing by 100. Since `DISCOUNT_RATE` is stored as a decimal fraction (0.10 = 10%), the extra division by 100 was causing the discount to be calculated as 0.1% instead of 10%. I removed the `/ 100` so the discount is now correctly calculated as `total_cents * DISCOUNT_RATE`.
